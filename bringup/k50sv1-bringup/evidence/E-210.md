plus three seconds of preview took 1701 ms. The wrong autofocus driver and absent
flash-controller accesses were confirmed software defects and repaired. With
the repaired kernel, the earlier lit-room samples were 424 ms steady, 416 ms
after restart and 618 ms with forced flash. [Boot-only experiment](device-review-20260907/kernel-live/receipt.json).

and 209 ms front**, with roughly 104/105/73 ms more for the JPEG callback.

2560×1920 front sizes. These are individual samples under changing room and
system conditions, not a latency distribution or controlled dark-scene comparison.
They demonstrate a software contribution to the original problem; they do not
establish a hardware ceiling or universally eliminate long delays. Two attempts
blocked by a location-permission dialog are retained as invalid fixtures.

The final Tier-3 samples are **570 ms rear steady, 633 ms rear after restart,
and 327 ms front**. All three JPEGs decode at the same correct rear/front sizes.

samples, with no universal latency or subjective image-quality claim.
