Physical camera-hole / status-bar overlay decision
audited_at=2026-08-23T06:02:00+02:00
device=720x1560 LCD, physical/product density 320 dpi
live_user_override=256 dpi

## Proven geometry contract

Current Lineage uses AOSP's 24 dp status bar. With the owner's persisted
256-dpi display override, WindowManager reports a 38-pixel top/stable inset:

    mStable=[0,38][720,1560]

Stock framework-res for this exact build uses:

    status_bar_height_portrait = 40dp
    status_bar_height_landscape = @status_bar_height_portrait
    quick_qs_offset_height = 48dp
    config_mainBuiltInDisplayCutout = ""
    config_mainBuiltInDisplayCutoutRectApproximation =
        @config_mainBuiltInDisplayCutout

Stock MtkSystemUI references the framework status-bar height directly, has
zero status-bar top padding and no OEM hardcoded camera-hole spacer. Its
cutout spacer follows ordinary AOSP DisplayCutout state.

The source fix therefore overlays only:

    status_bar_height_portrait = 40dp
    status_bar_height_landscape = 40dp

It intentionally leaves quick_qs at the existing AOSP 48 dp default and leaves
all cutout path/rect/fill resources unchanged. At product/default 320 dpi the
bar is 80 px, exactly matching Stock. At the current user-forced 256 dpi it is
64 px. User density state is not converted into a product resource.

## Why no synthetic DisplayCutout path is declared

Live WindowManager reports empty DisplayCutout bounds and zero safe insets.
No cutout emulation overlay is active. The safe screencap was black because
the handset was asleep; even an illuminated framebuffer capture cannot show a
physical LCD aperture because it captures rendered pixels, not the camera and
opaque panel hole. The workspace contains no perspective-corrected front-panel
photograph from this exact handset.

Inventing a center/radius from another clone would alter application safe
insets, rotation, SystemUI center spacing and letterboxing with false geometry.
The clean first build reproduces Stock's reserved height only. A real cutout
path requires a square-on photograph containing all active display corners,
perspective correction to 720x1560, and measured center/radius including the
black decoration ring plus a small safety margin.

## Static validation

The targeted framework-res build succeeds. Its final resource table contains:

    dimen/status_bar_height_portrait  40.000000dp
    dimen/status_bar_height_landscape 40.000000dp
    dimen/quick_qs_offset_height      48.000000dp
      land -> @status_bar_height_landscape
    string/config_mainBuiltInDisplayCutout ""
    bool/config_fillMainBuiltInDisplayCutout false

## Post-flash gates

* Record `wm density`: expect stable top 64 px at 256 dpi or 80 px at 320 dpi.
* DisplayCutout bounds/safeInsets must remain zero until physical measurement.
* Inspect time, notifications, signal and battery against the real hole on
  white/bright backgrounds, lock screen, heads-up notifications and open QS.
* Test left, center and right Lineage clock modes. Center clock may collide
  because no measured center cutout is declared; keep the default left clock
  if it does.
* Rotate ordinary and fullscreen applications through 90 and 270 degrees.
  Stock also lacks a side safe inset in landscape; any unacceptable physical
  obstruction is evidence to measure and implement a real path later.
